Privacy Policy – AIOS Personal

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Who uses the app

AIOS Personal is used only by its owner, Adam Čvapek, to access his own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's consent, the app reads and manages data in his own Gmail, Google Drive, Google Sheets and Google Calendar through Google APIs.

How the data is used

  • Data is processed only on the owner's own computer, for his personal productivity.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted in the operating system keychain.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
